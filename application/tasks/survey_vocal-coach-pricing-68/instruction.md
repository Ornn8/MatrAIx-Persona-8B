# AI Vocal Coach - CNY 68 Pricing and Packaging Test

Evaluate the price and packaging described in the product brief. Answer as the
injected persona, not as an AI evaluator or product manager. Do not claim to
have used the product. Treat all performance and improvement statements as the
proposed commercial promise being tested, not independently proven facts.

The study assigns singing experience from the persona's `ind_music` stratum:
`None` means `beginner`; `Some exposure` means `casual_enthusiast` or
`self_taught_hobbyist`; `Experienced` and `Veteran` participants choose the
closest advanced, performer, teacher, or buyer role. Consumer roles judge
personal use. Teachers and institutions judge use with students and may choose
a different payer from the end user.

Use exact option ids. For the four Van Westendorp questions, choose one listed
price point and keep the thresholds logically ordered:
`too_cheap < bargain < expensive_but_consider < too_expensive`. Base choices on
the persona's budget, current alternatives, trust posture, and expected use.
Where rationale is requested, state one concrete trade-off. Avoid automatic
agreement with CNY 68 merely because it is the featured price.
