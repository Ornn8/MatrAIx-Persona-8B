# AI Vocal Coach - proposed launch package and price

## Product and first-use experience

This is a future-product pricing test, not a claim that participants have used
a working product. The product is a complete, structured vocal-learning system
aligned with mainstream vocal-training concepts. On first use, a learner builds
a baseline voice sample. The system identifies the single highest-priority area
to improve, shows the exact original audio evidence clip, explains the finding,
and recommends a targeted exercise and one-week plan.

The commercial promise is that diligent completion of the first week's plan
should produce a noticeable improvement in that specific issue. This promise
is part of the package respondents must judge; it is not independent proof of
accuracy or outcomes.

## Included in the CNY 68 monthly core package

- A complete progressive vocal curriculum, not diagnosis alone.
- Baseline voice assessment and evidence-linked technical diagnosis.
- One prioritized improvement target at a time and matching exercises.
- Personalized weekly practice plans and progress comparison against the user's baseline.
- Original evidence-clip playback instead of only a score.
- Clear separation of high-confidence findings from issues that may exist.
- Explicit acknowledgement when confidence is too low to determine an issue.
- A way to mark and report disagreement when a teacher's opinion conflicts.
- Immediate stop guidance for pain, persistent hoarseness, or unusual fatigue,
  with referral to an appropriate human professional.
- Recordings private by default; model-improvement use requires separate opt-in.

## Trial and featured price

The complete core package is free for the first seven days. No charge is made
during the trial. After the trial it renews at **CNY 68 per month**, cancellable
before renewal. The pricing test compares this package with quarterly,
pay-per-analysis, lower-feature, and premium alternatives. Treat all listed
plans as hypothetical research choices.

Useful comparison anchors may include a private vocal lesson, a KTV membership
or singing-entertainment subscription, and a general practice app. These are
not assumed substitutes: respondents should decide which comparison is
personally meaningful.
