# AI Vocal Coach - Full Product Concept v3

Evaluate the complete first-release concept described in `input/context.md`, then
answer every item in `input/questionnaire.yaml` as the injected persona. This is
a concept test, not a claim that the respondent has used a finished product.

The study assigns singing experience from the persona's `ind_music` stratum so
the 200-person quota is reproducible:

- `None`: answer as a beginner with little or no structured vocal training.
- `Some exposure`: answer as a KTV/social singer, casual enthusiast, or
  self-taught hobbyist.
- `Experienced`: answer as the closest serious student, advanced hobbyist,
  creator, performer, choir/band singer, junior teacher, or music-adjacent buyer.
- `Veteran`: answer as the closest senior performer, vocal teacher/coach, voice
  professional, studio/school/choir buyer, or highly experienced singer.

Treat that assigned singing role as part of the research scenario even when the
base persona has few singing-specific attributes. Consumer roles judge personal
use; teachers, coaches, and buyers judge use with students or clients. Keep the
persona's trust posture, privacy expectations, budget sensitivity, habits, and
professional incentives intact.

The concept being tested includes all of these facts and they must not be
silently omitted or weakened in your evaluation:

1. First use builds a baseline voice profile and effective-range map from guided
   voice samples.
2. The system identifies the single highest-priority issue, explains it, plays
   the exact original evidence clip, and recommends a directly matched exercise.
3. Findings are explicitly separated into high-confidence diagnoses and
   tentative "may be present" observations. When confidence is too low, the
   product says it cannot determine the issue instead of pretending certainty.
4. This is a complete, progressive teaching curriculum aligned with mainstream
   vocal-training concepts, not a one-off score or isolated exercise library.
5. The first practice plan focuses on one specific issue and is designed so that
   correct practice can produce a clearly noticeable improvement within one week.
6. The first week is free. Continued personal access costs CNY 68 per month.
7. Users may mark disagreement and send feedback when a diagnosis conflicts with
   a teacher's judgement; the product does not claim to replace a qualified teacher.
8. Pain, persistent hoarseness, or meaningful vocal fatigue causes training to
   stop, with advice to seek appropriate qualified human help.
9. Recordings and reports are private by default; model-improvement use requires
   separate per-recording opt-in consent that can be withdrawn.

Use the listed option ids for choice questions and a whole number for Likert
questions. Where `askRationale` is enabled, give one concrete reason grounded in
the persona. Do not invent unlisted features, claim firsthand use, or assume the
diagnosis is accurate merely because the concept describes safeguards. Judge
value, credibility, realistic trial behavior, CNY 68 subscription intent,
skepticism, and role fit independently.
