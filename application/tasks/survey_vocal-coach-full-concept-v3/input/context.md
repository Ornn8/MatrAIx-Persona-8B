# Complete first-release concept brief: AI vocal coach

**Stage:** concept test. Assume the following product experience is available as
described, but do not assume its diagnostic accuracy or one-week outcomes have
already been independently proven.

## Positioning and intended users

The product is a candid AI vocal coach for adult beginners, KTV and social
singers, self-taught and serious hobbyists, vocal students and music majors,
creators and performers, choir or band singers, vocal teachers/coaches, other
voice professionals, and studios, schools, or choirs. It is designed to automate
time-consuming listening, diagnosis, and practice planning while complementing,
not replacing, a qualified human teacher.

Unlike entertainment karaoke scoring, the product's purpose is improvement. It
listens to singing, identifies the most important technical issue, shows where
the evidence occurs, explains why it matters, assigns suitable practice, and
tracks whether the user's voice is changing.

## First use and the first week

On first use, the learner completes guided voice samples to establish a baseline
voice profile. The assessment distinguishes notes that can merely be produced
from the comfortable and technically usable range, marks strained or unsafe
areas conservatively, and identifies likely register-transition points.

The first report then:

1. selects the single issue that most needs improvement now;
2. explains what the issue is, what it sounds like, and why it may happen;
3. plays the exact original audio evidence segment at the relevant timestamp,
   rather than presenting only a score;
4. labels a well-supported result as a **high-confidence diagnosis** and a less
   certain signal as **may be present**;
5. explicitly says **cannot determine from this sample** when confidence is too
   low, instead of forcing a conclusion; and
6. opens a directly matched lesson and exercise plan.

The plan deliberately focuses on that specific issue and is designed so that,
when the exercises are performed correctly, the learner can hear or feel a
clearly noticeable improvement within one week. The first week is free. After
the free week, continued personal access costs **CNY 68 per month**.

## Complete teaching system

This is not only a diagnostic report or a collection of tips. It includes a
complete, progressive curriculum aligned with mainstream vocal-training
concepts. Courses cover such connected dimensions as breath management, vocal
fold closure, resonance, registers and register transitions, and safe high-note
work. Prerequisites unlock in a sensible order instead of encouraging learners
to skip ahead.

Each short lesson contains explanation, a sung demonstration, guided repetition,
and immediate comparison feedback: what improved, what is still missing, and
what to notice next time. The system automatically schedules the next practice
from the diagnosis, while the learner can adjust the plan and a teacher can
review or override it. Daily goals, reminders, streaks, and a non-punitive return
after missed days support adherence.

## Reports, progress, and disagreement

Reports show progress before problems, prioritize one issue by default, keep
secondary findings collapsed, and link each presented conclusion to replayable
original evidence. Progress is tracked by technique dimension across lessons,
not as one entertainment-style song score. The first release includes technique
growth curves, a current skill view, effective-range history, and practice
check-ins.

Users can mark a diagnosis as disputed and send feedback when it conflicts with
their own perception or a teacher's judgement. A teacher can review the evidence
and report, comment on it, and adjust the next practice. The product treats such
conflict as feedback to resolve, not proof that the user or teacher is wrong.

## Safety and uncertainty

The product does not treat style differences as technical defects and does not
encourage imitation of risky effects or extreme notes. It monitors signs of
fatigue and risky phonation conservatively. If the user reports or shows pain,
persistent hoarseness, or meaningful vocal fatigue, the session stops rather
than continuing the exercise and recommends appropriate qualified human help.
Uncertain evidence is labeled as uncertain; very low-confidence findings are not
turned into authoritative diagnoses or training instructions.

## Privacy and first-release scope

Practice recordings, reports, and progress data are private by default. Data-use
terms are shown separately and clearly. Model-improvement use requires voluntary,
per-recording opt-in consent; declining does not remove core functionality,
consent can be withdrawn, and deletion can be requested.

The first release consists of accounts and consent, guided range/baseline
assessment, the evidence-based diagnostic report, the complete curriculum and
matched practice system, growth tracking, check-ins, and the safety behavior
above. Do not assume later-phase song libraries, accompaniment separation,
social feeds, marketplaces, or full institution administration are included.

## What this study must reveal

Judge whether the complete journey solves a real problem; which element is the
core selling point; whether the evidence and uncertainty design earns trust;
whether a one-week improvement promise is credible enough to try; realistic
behavior during a free first week; willingness to subscribe at CNY 68 per month;
what would sustain or cancel a subscription; the biggest remaining suspicion;
and how these answers differ between beginners, hobbyists, experienced users,
veterans, teachers, and buyers.
