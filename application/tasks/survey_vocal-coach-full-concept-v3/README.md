# AI Vocal Coach - Full Product Concept v3

This independent MatrAIx survey tests the complete first-release AI vocal coach
journey. It includes baseline voice profiling, one prioritized diagnosis,
original evidence playback, explicit confidence and uncertainty, a complete
progressive curriculum, a matched one-week practice plan, a free first week,
CNY 68 monthly pricing, teacher-conflict feedback, and stop-training safety.

## Default 200-person study

The task reuses the sampling strategy of
`survey_vocal-coach-concept-feedback`: 50 personas from each `ind_music` stratum.

| Research quota | Persona proxy | Runs |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Beginner / little formal exposure | `ind_music=None` | 50 |
| KTV, casual, or self-taught hobbyist | `ind_music=Some exposure` | 50 |
| Experienced singer, student, performer, or junior professional | `ind_music=Experienced` | 50 |
| Veteran singer, teacher, coach, or professional buyer | `ind_music=Veteran` | 50 |
| **Total** |  | **200** |

The first two strata are 100 of 200 participants. All sampled personas are
adults interested in or passionate about music, with varied trust and spending
postures. The stratum is a reproducible research-role proxy, not an assumed
answer.

## Instrument

The 15 questions measure role and current behavior, unmet need, complete-concept
value, core selling point, evidence/confidence trust, curriculum value, one-week
credibility, realistic free-week behavior, CNY 68 subscription intent, retention,
remaining skepticism, teacher conflict, safety response, and a role-specific open
reaction.

Interpret simulated answers as directional hypotheses. Compare all major metrics
by `ind_music` and `q0_research_role`; do not treat persona simulation as market
conversion evidence or as proof of diagnostic accuracy, safety, or real one-week
improvement.
