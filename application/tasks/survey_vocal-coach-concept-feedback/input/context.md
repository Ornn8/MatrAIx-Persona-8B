# AI Vocal Coach - early product concept

**Stage:** concept only. The product has not been built or launched, and no
accuracy claim is being tested in this survey.

## Intended users

The concept may serve adult beginners, KTV and social singers, self-taught and
serious hobbyists, vocal students, creators and performers, vocal teachers or
coaches, and studios or educational institutions. It is acceptable to conclude
that the product is not useful for a particular role.

## Core promise

The platform would listen to a user's singing, identify the most important
technical issue, show the audio evidence behind that diagnosis, explain why it
matters, and recommend one matching exercise. The goal is more directed and
measurable practice, not replacement of a good vocal teacher.

## First-release concept

- A guided assessment that maps comfortable, usable, strained, and unsafe parts
  of the vocal range.
- An evidence-based diagnostic report with a short playback clip, the main
  issue, an explanation, a confidence indication, and one matching exercise.
- Short practice plans for breath, closure, resonance, register transitions,
  and high notes.
- Progress views based on technique dimensions and range history rather than a
  single entertainment-style song score.
- Conservative fatigue and risky-phonation warnings.
- For professionals, an optional student-facing report that a teacher can
  review, annotate, or use between lessons.

## Not promised for the first release

Song libraries, accompaniment separation, key transposition, social sharing,
community, live teacher marketplaces, and full studio administration may be
considered later. Respondents should not assume these exist.

## Evidence, safety, and limits

A diagnosis should point to the exact audio segment that triggered it, explain
the observable signal in plain language, show uncertainty, and avoid presenting
style preferences as technical defects. The product must not encourage users to
push through pain, fatigue, screams, or extreme high notes. It should recommend
professional human help when the evidence is uncertain or a health risk may be
present.

## Privacy concept

Recordings are private by default. Model-improvement use requires separate,
per-recording opt-in consent. Consent can be withdrawn, and the user can request
deletion. The product should state retention and processing clearly; these are
concept requirements, not proof that an implementation already meets them.

## Research question

Judge whether the concept solves a real problem, what creates or destroys trust,
whether the privacy model is acceptable, realistic trial and payment intent,
and what evidence would make the diagnosis credible. Do not evaluate model
accuracy or pretend to have seen a working demo.
