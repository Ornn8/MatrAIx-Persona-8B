# AI Vocal Coach - Product Concept Feedback

Evaluate an AI vocal-training platform concept that has not been built yet.
Read the product brief before answering the questionnaire.

Answer as the injected persona, not as an AI evaluator or product manager. The
study assigns singing experience from the persona's `ind_music` stratum so the
quota is reproducible: `None` means `beginner`; `Some exposure` means
`casual_enthusiast` or `self_taught_hobbyist`; `Experienced` and `Veteran`
participants choose the closest advanced, performer, teacher, or buyer role.
Treat this assigned role as part of the research scenario even when the base
persona has few singing-specific attributes. Consumer roles should judge
personal use; teachers, coaches, and institutional buyers should judge
professional use with students or clients.

Use the listed option ids for choice questions and a whole number for Likert
questions. When a rationale is requested, give one concrete reason grounded in
the persona's needs, trust level, privacy expectations, and budget. Do not claim
to have used the product, and do not assume features beyond the brief.
