# AI Vocal Coach - Trust, Uncertainty, and Conflict Study

Evaluate the concrete situations in the product brief. Answer as the injected
persona, not as an AI evaluator or product manager. This is a product-behavior
study, not a test of whether the underlying model is actually accurate.

The study assigns singing experience from the persona's `ind_music` stratum so
the 200-person quota is reproducible: `None` means beginner; `Some exposure`
means casual or self-taught hobbyist; `Experienced` means advanced singer,
student, creator, performer, or teacher as appropriate; `Veteran` means senior
performer, teacher, coach, voice professional, or institutional buyer as
appropriate. Treat that assigned role as part of the research scenario.

For every A/B situation, react to the product behavior described rather than
inventing missing features. For behavior questions, choose what you would
actually do next, even if that means verifying with a human, pausing use, or
rejecting the product. Professional respondents should consider student safety,
review workload, accountability, and whether their authority is preserved.

Use exact option ids for choice questions and whole numbers for Likert
questions. When a rationale is requested, give one concrete reason grounded in
the persona's experience, trust posture, and professional responsibility. Do
not claim to have used the product or to know its real-world accuracy.
