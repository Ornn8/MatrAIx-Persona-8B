# AI Vocal Coach - First-Week Journey Test

Read the first-week product scenario, then move through the questionnaire as if
you had just discovered the product. Answer as the injected persona and make a
realistic decision at every stage rather than acting as a product manager.

The study assigns singing experience from the persona's `ind_music` stratum so
the quota is reproducible: `None` means a beginner or low-exposure singer;
`Some exposure` means a casual, KTV, or self-taught hobbyist; `Experienced`
means an advanced learner, creator, performer, or music-adjacent professional;
and `Veteran` means a senior performer, teacher, coach, or institutional user.
Treat that assigned level as part of the research scenario.

At each stage, choose what you would most likely do. If you would stop, delay,
or reduce participation, select the option that contains the main reason. Do
not assume perfect diagnosis, guaranteed improvement, or features beyond the
brief. Use the listed option ids for choice questions. When a rationale is
requested, give one concrete reason grounded in the persona's habits, trust,
privacy needs, time, and budget.
