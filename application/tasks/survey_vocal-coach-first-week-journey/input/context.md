# AI Vocal Coach - first use and free first week

## Product and teaching system

Assume the product is a complete, structured vocal-learning system aligned with
mainstream vocal pedagogy, not merely a scoring tool. It covers progressive
teaching beyond the first week, but this study follows only the first-use and
first-week journey.

The first week is free. If the user continues after the free week, the proposed
personal subscription is CNY 68 per month and may be cancelled at any time.

## First-use journey

1. **Voice profile:** The user records several short guided samples in a quiet
   place. The samples establish a baseline of comfortable, usable, strained,
   and potentially unsafe vocal behaviour. Recordings are private by default.
2. **One priority:** The system identifies the single issue that most needs
   improvement first, instead of presenting a long list of scores.
3. **Evidence and uncertainty:** The report plays the exact original audio
   excerpt that supports the finding and explains it in plain language. It
   labels findings as either **high-confidence** or **possibly present**. When
   confidence is low, it states that it cannot determine the issue and requests
   a better sample or human review rather than pretending certainty.
4. **Teacher feedback:** If a teacher disagrees, the user can mark the conflict,
   preserve the teacher's view, and submit feedback. The product is intended to
   support, not silently overrule, qualified human instruction.
5. **One-week exercise:** The system recommends a focused exercise and a short
   seven-day plan for the priority issue. The plan is designed so that a
   suitable user can notice a clear issue-specific improvement within a week;
   improvement is not guaranteed for every person or condition.
6. **Safety:** Pain, persistent hoarseness, unusual fatigue, or worsening voice
   immediately stops the exercise. The product recommends qualified human or
   medical help instead of encouraging the user to push through symptoms.
7. **Day-7 retest:** The user repeats comparable samples, sees before-and-after
   evidence, and decides whether the improvement is meaningful enough to keep
   practising and subscribe.

## What this study measures

This is a scenario-based journey test, not a model-accuracy test. Evaluate the
effort, trust, safety, practice adherence, improvement threshold, and payment
decision at each step. A respondent may leave at any stage even though later
questions still ask what they would prefer under that hypothetical stage.
