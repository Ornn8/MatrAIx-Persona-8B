# AI Vocal Coach - two-week use simulation

## Product available in this scenario

The product is a complete, structured vocal-learning system aligned with
mainstream vocal-pedagogy concepts. It supplements rather than replaces a
qualified teacher. On first use, it establishes a basic voice sample, identifies
the single highest-priority area to improve, shows the exact source-audio clip
behind that judgment, and assigns a matching 3-10 minute exercise.

Reports separate **high-confidence findings** from **possible findings**. When
confidence is low, the product says it cannot determine the issue. Users can
mark disagreement, record a teacher's conflicting opinion, and send feedback.
Pain, persistent hoarseness, or unusual fatigue stops training immediately and
triggers a recommendation for qualified human care.

The first seven days are free. The plan is designed so that a suitable,
consistently completed exercise can show a noticeable change in the targeted
issue within one week. This diary supplies a specific improvement event for the
simulation; it is not an external efficacy claim. Continuing the full program
after the free week costs **CNY 68 per month**.

Recordings are private by default. Model-improvement use requires separate,
per-recording opt-in. Users can withdraw consent and request deletion.

## Diary rules

Imagine that the persona installed the product for a genuine singing goal.
Each day presents what the product or user experienced. Choose the action they
would actually take next, including skipping, shortening, pausing safely,
subscribing, collaborating, sharing, reassessing, or leaving. Do not optimize
answers for the company.

The instrument measures behavioral proxies, not stated enthusiasm:

- Day 1 activation and first exercise completion.
- Day 7 retained use and first reassessment.
- Day 8 paid continuation at CNY 68/month.
- Day 14 retained paid use and second reassessment.
- Completed-practice-day band, recovery after misses, and reminder response.
- Continuation or churn after difficulty, visible improvement, repetition, and
  a plateau.
- Safe pause versus true churn after a voice-safety warning.
- Evidence sharing, teacher collaboration, disagreement feedback, and retest
  behavior.
