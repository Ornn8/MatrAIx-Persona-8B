# AI Vocal Coach - Two-week Behavioral Diary

Simulate actual product behavior across the 14-day diary in order. This is not
an opinion survey: for every day, choose the action the injected persona would
most realistically take after the event described in the product brief.

Use the persona's schedule, habits, budget, trust level, privacy expectations,
and music experience. The assigned `ind_music` stratum is the reproducible
singing-experience proxy: `None` means a beginner; `Some exposure` means a KTV,
casual, or self-taught hobbyist; `Experienced` means an advanced student,
creator, performer, or music-adjacent professional; `Veteran` means a senior
performer, teacher, coach, or institutional user. Treat that assigned role as
part of the scenario even when the base persona has little singing detail.

Maintain state across days. Once you choose `stop_using`, `abandon_onboarding`,
or a non-subscription path at the Day 8 paywall, use the applicable inactive
option on later days. A safety pause is not churn: after pain, persistent
hoarseness, or fatigue, a persona may rest or seek human help and resume only
when cleared. Never select unsafe continued training merely to make retention
look better.

The diary describes product events, not proven real-world outcomes. Accept each
event as the scenario shown to the persona, but do not invent extra features or
claim the model is clinically validated. Use exact option ids for choice
questions. Keep the final state, practice-day band, reassessment count, and
30-day action consistent with the preceding daily choices. When a rationale is
requested, give one concrete behavioral reason rather than general product
advice.
